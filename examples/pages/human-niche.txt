Title: Notes from repairing a 1978 tube radio

I spent Saturday tracing the heater wiring on a Zenith with a cold 50C5.
The schematic in Rider's is close but the production board routes the
dropper resistor under the tuner can, which nobody mentions online. After
replacing the wax coupling cap on the volume control the hum dropped and
the dial lamps came back. This is not a universal guide — just what I
found on this chassis so the next person does not dig for an hour.
